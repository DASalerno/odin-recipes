Nonna's Pizza

Description

A simple, yet delicious focaccia-style pizza topped with cherry tomatoes and cheese.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of flour
  • 2.5 tsp of yeast
  • 2.5 tsp of salt
  • 1 tsp of sugar
  • 1.5 cup of lukewarm water
  • 3 tbsp of olive oil
  • 1.5 cups of diced cherry tomatoes
  • 1 cup of shredded cheese

Steps

  1. In a mixing bowl, integrate the flour, yeast, salt, and sugar.
  2. While mixing, slowly pour one cup of water and the olive oil.
  3. Add remaining water as needed and continue mixing until dough reaches desired consistency.
  4. Cover the mixing bowl and let the dough rest for at least one hour.
  5. Spread a small amount of olive oil across a pan.
  6. Place the dough in the pan and gradually spread the dough across the pan.
  7. Place the pan in the oven to let the dough rest at least another half hour.
  8. Remove the pan from the oven, and preheat the oven to 450℉.
  9. The dough will likely have contracted somewhat while resting; spread it out across the pan as needed.
  10. Distribute the diced cherry tomatoes evenly atop the dough, being careful not to let too many clump together.
  11. Place the pan in the oven for 10-12 minutes.
  12. Remove the pan and distribute the shredded cheese evenly atop the pizza.
  13. Place the pan in the oven for another 8-10 minutes, or until the bottom of the pizza is a nice golden-brown.
