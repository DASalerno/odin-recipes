Sausage Gravy

Description

A savory gravy that pairs wonderfully with some lightly buttered biscuits.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb of sage breakfast sausage
  • 0.25 cup of flour
  • 2.5 cups of whole milk
  • Desired seasonings

Steps

  1. Brown sausage over medium high heat while breaking it up.
  2. Sprinkle flour over the sausage evenly, then stir until flour is absorbed.
  3. Slowly pour milk in, stirring as you go.
  4. Add desired seasonings.
  5. Cook and stir frequently until mixture is thickened to desired consistency.
