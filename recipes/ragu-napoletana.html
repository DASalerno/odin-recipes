Ragù alla Napoletana

Description

Cooked with pork over many hours, this sauce is abundantly flavorful. Get it started early in the morning and enjoy a perfect Sunday lunch.

Ingredients

  • Half an onion, diced
  • 2 stalks of celery, diced
  • 2-3 carrots, diced
  • 2-3 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 lb of boneless pork shoulder
  • 3 Italian pork sausages
  • 7-8 cups of tomato sauce
  • 7-8 cups of water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Rosemary
  • Basil
  • Oregano
  • Nutmeg

Steps

  1. Heat a small amount of olive oil in a large pot on low heat.
  2. Add the onions, celery, and carrots and cook until soft. This is known as a soffritto.
  3. Add the minced garlic and let cook for a couple minutes.
  4. While the garlic is cooking, turn the heat up to medium and then sear the meats on each side.
  5. When garlic is a light golden color, increase to medium-high heat and add the tomato sauce and water.
  6. Bring the sauce to a boil and then turn the heat down to low to let simmer, stirring occassionally.
  7. Add 2 bay leaves and let the sauce thicken to the desired consistency. This will take several hours.
  8. Once the sauce has nearly reached the desired consistency, add remaining seasonings as desired.
